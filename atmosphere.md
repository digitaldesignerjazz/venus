# Venus Atmosphere at 50 km

## Pressure Profile

| Altitude | Pressure | Temperature |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Surface | 92 bar | 464°C |
| 50 km | ~1 bar | ~70°C |
| 60 km | ~0.5 bar | ~30°C |
| 70 km | ~0.2 bar | ~0°C |

## Composition (bulk atmosphere)

- CO2: 96.5%
- N2: 3.5%
- Trace: SO2, Ar, H2O, CO, He, Ne

## Cloud Layer

- 47–70 km: sulfuric acid aerosols
- Droplet concentration increases toward 50–55 km
- Above ~65 km, acid concentration drops significantly

## Habitability Notes

- 50 km: pressure OK, temp survivable with cooling, acid present
- 55–60 km: less acid, cooler, but lower pressure (needs some pressurization)
- Sweet spot for floating habitats: 50–55 km

## Power

- Solar flux at Venus orbit: ~2600 W/m² (1.9x Earth)
- Above clouds: direct sunlight, minimal atmospheric attenuation
- Solar is the obvious primary power source
