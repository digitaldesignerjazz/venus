# Acid-Resistant Materials for Venus Platforms

## Sulfuric Acid Environment

Venus cloud layer: ~47–70 km altitude, concentrated H2SO4 droplets.

## Recommended Stack

1. **Outer hull / envelope**: PTFE-coated fabric or PTFE film laminates
2. **Primary structure**: Glass-fiber composite with PTFE or ceramic coating
3. **Gondola / pressure vessel**: 316L stainless steel or Hastelloy C-276
4. **Sensitive electronics**: Ceramic housings, PTFE-sealed connectors
5. **Seals and gaskets**: PTFE or Viton (FKM) — Viton resists H2SO4 well

## What NOT to use

- Bare aluminum (any alloy) — reacts with H2SO4
- Uncoated carbon fiber — galvanic corrosion risk with metal parts
- Standard rubber seals — swell and degrade

## Testing Protocol (suggested)

- 96-hour immersion in 98% H2SO4 at 70°C
- UV exposure cycling (Venus solar flux ~1.9x Earth)
- Thermal cycling 30–90°C
