# Venus

Venus colonization research — Esslinger & Co. / Nexus ecosystem.

## The Sweet Spot

At approximately 50 km altitude above Venus:
- **Atmospheric pressure**: Earth-like (~1 bar)
- **Temperature**: ~70°C (some sources cite ~50°C)
- **Gravity**: 0.9 g — near Earth normal
- **Day length**: 243 Earth days (retrograde rotation)

This is the only region of Venus with habitable surface conditions for humans without pressurized suits.

## The Problem: Sulfuric Acid Clouds

The atmosphere contains concentrated sulfuric acid (H2SO4) clouds. Any structure must resist this.

### Material Resistance

| Material | Resistance | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Aluminum | Poor | Dissolved by sulfuric acid |
| Aluminum foil | Poor | Same issue, worse surface area |
| **PTFE (Teflon)** | Excellent | Chemically inert to H2SO4 |
| Glass-fiber reinforced polymer + acid-resistant coating | Good | Structural option |
| **Hastelloy** (Ni-Mo alloy) | Excellent | Holds even concentrated acid; heavy and expensive |
| **316L stainless steel** | Good | Option for gondola/structural parts |
| Ceramics | Excellent | For most sensitive components |

## Concepts

- **Airships**: Buoyant platforms floating at 50 km, above the acid clouds but in the sweet spot pressure zone
- **Floating platforms / cities**: Larger semi-permanent structures
- Not ground cities — the surface is 460°C and 92 bar

## NASA HAVOC Reference

See **havoc-whitepaper.md** for the full whitepaper on NASA's High Altitude Venus Operational Concept (Arney & Jones, 2015) — the crewed airship study that defined the 50 km sweet spot, the five-phase exploration roadmap, FEP-Teflon solar protection (90% transmittance after 30 days in H2SO4), and the Entry-Descent-Inflation sequence.

Key HAVOC specs:
- Robotic airship: 31 m × 8 m
- Crewed airship (Phase III): 129 m × 34 m, >1,000 m² solar array, ~70 t payload
- 30-day crewed stay at 50 km; Phase IV extends to 1 year; Phase V = permanent cloud city
- Total mission time ~440 days (110 out, 30 stay, 300 back)
- Launch windows every ~1.5 years

## Open Questions

- Long-term PTFE degradation under UV and thermal cycling
- Power generation (solar is viable above clouds)
- Water sourcing (atmospheric CO2 + hydrogen electrolysis?)
- Radiation shielding (Venus has no magnetic field)
- Cryogenic boil-off management for ascent vehicle propellants (HAVOC open problem — potential Nexus cooling contribution)
- In-situ resource utilization from the CO2 atmosphere
